Hi, we’re Intrepid Toad! We’re a pair of game developers (alt-kreation and Pijove) from Ireland that work together to make small games, tools, and articles.

You’ll find stuff we’ve worked on together here! Some of our releases include a Unity plugin to add Platonic Solids to the base 3D Objects and a small game called Pigeons Aren’t Pests.

But feel free to check out our individual pages too!

On Pijove’s side of the website you’ll find absolute rakes of articles about FPS and RTS games, balancing games, and serious games (rehabilitation and training).

On alt-kreation’s side you’ll find games and articles that aren’t FPS or RTS!
